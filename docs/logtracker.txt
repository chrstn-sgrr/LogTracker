LogTracker (working title was Logbook prior) is a minimalist mobile fitness app designed to combine workout logging and nutrition tracking into one fast, straightforward experience without the clutter and unnecessary restrictions found in many existing fitness apps. The free core product focuses on frictionless workout tracking with customizable exercises, routines, previous-performance comparisons, notes, and progress history, alongside simple calorie and macronutrient tracking with fast manual food entry and barcode scanning. Its philosophy is to help users “record today and beat yesterday” while spending as little time interacting with the app as possible. A future optional premium tier would build on the data users already log by providing intelligent progression, recovery, and exercise suggestions based on performance and user feedback, while keeping the user—not the app—in control.
